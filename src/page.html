How we work

Small, provable steps.

You see a working result early, and you only commit to more once it has earned it.

  1. Map

    Week 1 · free

    We sit with the people doing the work, list every manual step, and estimate the hours each one costs.

  2. Prove

    Weeks 2–4

    We automate the highest-value step first and run it alongside the old way, so you can compare results directly.

  3. Ship

    Per scope

    We roll out the rest in priority order, with documentation and training for the team that will own it.

  4. Run

    Ongoing

    We monitor, fix and extend what we built. You get a monthly note on what ran, what broke and what we changed.

Questions

Before you ask.

What size of business do you work with?

Small and mid-sized teams, usually 5 to 500 people, where a few people lose hours every week to repetitive work. You don't need an IT department to work with us.

What if our task isn't listed on this site?

Send it anyway. The services and industries here are examples, not limits. If a task is manual and repeated, we can almost always automate it or cut it down a lot, and we'll tell you honestly in the first call if we can't.

Do you work with marketing agencies?

Yes. We connect to the Meta and Google Ads accounts you manage (with your clients' permission) and automate reporting, alerts, lead routing and conversion tracking across all of them at once.

Do we have to replace our existing software?

Usually not. Most of our work connects and automates the tools you already pay for. We only suggest new software when the existing tool is the actual problem.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Code, data and accounts stay in your name, and we document everything so another team could take it over.

How do you handle our data?

We work inside your accounts wherever possible, use access that's limited to the job, and sign an NDA before we see anything sensitive. AI features are set up so your data isn't used to train public models.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope. The audit gives you a fixed quote for each piece of work along with the time it saves, so you can decide whether it's worth it before spending anything.
