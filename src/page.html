Data pipeline · live

India Card Monitor

A public dashboard tracking India's credit card, debit card and UPI payments, bank by bank. The Reserve Bank of India publishes the figures as one Excel file a month, in layouts that have changed several times since 2011. NPCI publishes the UPI figures separately.

What it handles
Every monthly file since April 2011, more than 180 of them, covering 86 banks whose names appear in 190 different spellings.
How it runs
Every Monday and Thursday a scheduled job checks both sources, downloads anything new or revised, re-processes every file and republishes the dashboard.
Manual steps
None.
Why it matters
It is the same job as a monthly MIS report or a client ad report: files arrive from different sources in changing formats, and a finished report comes out with nobody touching it.

Questions

Before you ask.

What does it cost?

The first week, the audit, is free. It ends with a fixed quote for each piece of work, next to the hours that task costs you today, so you decide item by item before spending anything. Once something is live, a fixed monthly fee covers hosting, monitoring and fixes.

Who will we be dealing with?

nullshift is a new, small studio based in India. You talk directly to the people doing the work, with no account managers and no hand-offs. We introduce ourselves properly in our first reply, and nothing starts, including any access to your data, without a written agreement.

Who owns what you build?

Your data and your accounts are yours, always. The code is ours: we host and run it for you, and the monthly fee keeps it monitored and fixed. If you want to take it in-house, you can buy the code outright at a price agreed in writing before work starts. If you leave without buying it, we export your data and switch the automation off.

Do we have to replace our existing software?

Usually not. The point is to connect and automate the tools you already pay for. We only suggest new software when the existing tool is the actual problem.

How do you handle our data?

We connect to your accounts with access that's limited to the job, keep your data only as long as the automation needs it, and sign an NDA before we see anything sensitive. AI features are set up so your data isn't used to train public models.

What access do you need to our clients' ad accounts?

Reporting, alerts and dashboards need read access only, granted through your Meta Business Manager and Google Ads manager account with your clients' permission. You can remove it at any time. Anything that changes a campaign, such as bulk ad creation, is separate and stays off until you approve it.

We already use Looker Studio or a reporting tool. Why would we need you?

You may not. If a subscription tool already does the job, keep it, and we'll say so. We're useful for what those tools leave to your team: reports in your own format, alerts tuned to each client's budget, leads that must reach a client's CRM, and naming rules nobody has time to police.

What size of business is this for?

Teams of roughly 5 to 500 people, where a few people lose hours every week to the same task. You don't need an IT department to work with us.
