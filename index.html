Questions

Before you ask

What does it cost?

The first week, the audit, is free. It ends with a fixed quote for each piece of work, next to the hours that task costs you today. Once something is live, a fixed monthly fee covers hosting, monitoring and fixes.

Who owns what you build?

Your data and your accounts are yours, always. The code is ours: we host and run it for you. If you want it in-house, you can buy the code outright at a price agreed in writing before work starts. If you leave without buying it, we export your data and switch the automation off.

How do you handle our data and ad accounts?

Access limited to the job, an NDA before we see anything sensitive, and your data kept only as long as the automation needs it. Reports and alerts on ad accounts use read-only access, granted through your Business Manager, which you can remove at any time. AI features never train public models on your data.

Who will we be dealing with?

A new, small studio based in India. You talk directly to the people doing the work. We introduce ourselves properly in our first reply, and nothing starts, including any access, without a written agreement.
